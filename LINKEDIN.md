# LinkedIn Jobs as a complementary source

The tracker currently has no LinkedIn Jobs ingestion connector. The links below open searches in your LinkedIn account. Their saved alerts are separate from Telegram alerts.

| Search | Link |
| --- | --- |
| Software internships | [Search LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/search/?keywords=software+engineer+internship&location=Singapore&f_TPR=r604800&sortBy=DD) |
| Graduate software roles | [Search LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/search/?keywords=software+engineer+graduate&location=Singapore&f_TPR=r604800&sortBy=DD) |
| Data internships | [Search LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/search/?keywords=data+engineer+internship&location=Singapore&f_TPR=r604800&sortBy=DD) |
| Quant research internships | [Search LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/search/?keywords=quantitative+research+internship&location=Singapore&f_TPR=r604800&sortBy=DD) |
| Security internships | [Search LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/search/?keywords=cybersecurity+internship&location=Singapore&f_TPR=r604800&sortBy=DD) |
| Product internships | [Search LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/search/?keywords=product+management+internship&location=Singapore&f_TPR=r604800&sortBy=DD) |

1. Open a search and check that the location is Singapore. Adjust the experience filter to Internship / Entry level as appropriate.
2. Turn on the Job alert toggle on the search results page.
3. Under Jobs > Preferences > Job alerts, choose Daily and email/app notifications.
4. Use employer filters or separate employer searches for firms that lack direct coverage in [EMPLOYERS.md](EMPLOYERS.md).

LinkedIn supports daily or weekly alerts and up to 20 saved job alerts. See [LinkedIn help: Job alerts](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a511279) and [Manage job alerts](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1420165). Its Talent Solutions API integrations require LinkedIn approval; they are not a general public job-search feed ([official API access documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/talent/recruiter-system-connect)).
